# Mami Momos Design Library — Visual Direction

## Three possible approaches

### Theme Name: Rajasthan Atelier Archive
**Very Brief Intro:** An editorial design archive inspired by Jaipur’s craft traditions: warm paper, ink, paprika, and a precisely structured gallery. It should feel like opening a well-kept studio folio rather than operating a generic admin dashboard.

**Probability:** 0.06

### Theme Name: Midnight Paste-Up Room
**Very Brief Intro:** A dark, energetic art-direction desk with fluorescent production marks and a high-contrast campaign aesthetic. It channels an after-hours print studio where every design is ready to be reviewed under a work light.

**Probability:** 0.03

### Theme Name: Gallery Shelf System
**Very Brief Intro:** A quiet architectural catalog using cool mineral surfaces, controlled type, and museum-label details. The experience prioritizes clarity and careful curation over visual ornament.

**Probability:** 0.08

## Chosen approach: Rajasthan Atelier Archive

### Design Movement
Contemporary Indian editorial design meets **New Typography** exhibition cataloguing: tactile but restrained, with sharp graphic hierarchy and deliberate negative space.

### Core Principles
1. **Designs are treated as physical objects.** Thumbnails sit like printed sheets on a warm worktable, with real visual weight and a clear viewing state.
2. **Information is editorial, not administrative.** Labels, metadata, and controls borrow the language of a print catalogue instead of a conventional SaaS panel.
3. **The gallery has an asymmetric rhythm.** The selected project commands a spacious focus pane while the library remains visibly at hand.
4. **Every interaction clarifies provenance.** Front/back state and variants update immediately, with concise labels and no hidden controls.

### Color Philosophy
The base is a warm, paper-like cream that gives the digital workbench a human material quality. Deep **Jaipur indigo** supplies authoritative typography and frames, while paprika orange signals selection and forward action. Pale dusty rose and turmeric support distinction between projects without making the interface feel decorative.

### Layout Paradigm
An **open-folio layout**: a narrow vertical catalog strip on the left, a large live artboard in the centre, and a contextual specification rail on the right. On smaller screens, the catalogue collapses into a horizontal project shelf above the artboard, preserving the visual idea of browsing sheets.

### Signature Elements
1. A bold square **momo-fold mark** with four folded corners, used as both brand device and favicon.
2. Fine vertical rulers and small crop-mark motifs that indicate the artwork is part of a deliberate design archive.
3. Oversized project identifiers (for example, `01 / 06`) paired with quiet production metadata.

### Interaction Philosophy
Actions behave like flipping a physical print or swapping a proof sheet: selection is immediate, active controls are unmistakable, and transitions are understated. Project tiles preview on hover, while the selected tile has a persistent paprika edge and index marker.

### Animation
Use a 180–240 ms custom ease-out for state transitions. Artboards may fade and shift 6 px when the design side or variant changes; cards lift by 2 px on hover and settle with a 160 ms active press. Respect reduced-motion preferences by disabling non-essential transforms.

### Typography System
**DM Serif Display** is reserved for project titles and the archive statement, giving the dashboard an editorial voice. **Space Grotesk** handles all navigation, metadata, controls, and numerals with clean, compact utility. Titles use tight leading and selective italics; labels use uppercase, tracked text for print-room discipline.

### Brand Essence
**Mami Momos Design Library is a live, tactile archive for teams to inspect every campaign side, size, and colourway without losing the character of the work.**

Personality adjectives: **cultured, precise, appetising.**

### Brand Voice
Headlines are direct and observational; CTAs use action verbs from the language of print and curation. Microcopy is concise, helpful, and never generic.

> “Open the proof, not another folder.”

> “Flip the sheet. Compare the colourway.”

### Wordmark & Logo
The logo is a bold, text-free **momo fold**: four folded facets converge around a small square void, suggesting both a steamed dumpling and a folded paper proof. The wordmark pairs the low-contrast serif with a tight grotesk descriptor when text is used in context.

### Signature Brand Color
**Paprika Archive — #E84D2A.** It marks selected work, active controls, and key moments of motion across the product.
