# Design References

## Fusion Direction

The primary visual direction pairs the warm rose sandstone, stacked jharokha geometry, and perforated lattice rhythm associated with Jaipur’s Hawa Mahal with the calm timber framing, linear lattice, and generous negative space associated with Japanese machiya facades.

## Source Notes

- Sahapedia’s Hawa Mahal reference describes the iconic façade as a five-storey honeycomb composition of latticed windows and jharokhas, with a rose-toned material quality and an interplay of light through the screens. This informs the repeating arch motif, coral palette, and layered-screen treatment.
- A machiya architecture reference was inspected for its focus on a recognisable wooden lattice façade. The page was blocked before its full text could be retrieved, so only the broadly established visual cue of restrained timber lattice is used; no page-specific claims are reproduced.

## Intended UI Translation

- A warm coral-to-ink “crossroads” split hero that is distinct and editorial, not a literal monument collage.
- Abstract repeated arches on the Jaipur side and vertical timber/shoji lattice on the Japanese side.
- A bespoke “Pitch Tank” motif for entrepreneur-show energy, avoiding an imitation of any official Shark Tank logo or wordmark.
