## Pamphlet Implications

- The full menu is too large for a compact street flyer; the pamphlet should feature a **short curated selection** rather than the entire catalogue.
- Best lead categories for a coupon flyer appear to be **Steam Momo**, **Zinger Dumpling**, **Fried / Pan Fried / Chilli Momo**, and **Momos Burger** because they are the most brand-relevant and price-comprehensible at a glance.
- Offer-led copy can legitimately reference the brand’s **Shark Tank** association, but the precise wording should stay tasteful and not overclaim.
- The address should be shortened for on-fly readability while preserving full print details on at least one variation.
- The master format remains **A5 portrait** with strong contrast for Jaipur sunlight and heat.
