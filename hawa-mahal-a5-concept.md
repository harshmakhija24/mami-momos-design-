# Hawa Mahal Visitor Pamphlet — Variant 01

## Purpose

This is an **A5, double-sided handout concept** for visitors already around Hawa Mahal. Its job is to make the Mami Momos offer immediately legible on the street, while preserving a distinctive Jaipur-meets-Tokyo visual language.

## Front: `Pink City, Hot Momo`

The front holds a large Hawa Mahal facade illustration in paprika with indigo Tokyo-inspired roofline and lantern motifs layered behind it. The main copy reads **“Momos for the Pink City walk.”** It introduces the concept as a nearby street-food stop without making unverified distance or opening-hour claims. A clear featured panel is reserved for the local visitor offer and contains editable placeholders for address, QR, and price.

## Back: `Three Hawa Mahal Combos`

The back uses the same indigo, paprika, turmeric, and paper palette. It offers three editable combo blocks, grounded in names visible in the supplied menu:

| Combo label | Menu names used | Price state |
| --- | --- | --- |
| City Loop | Veg Steam Momo (6 pcs), Kadak Masala Chai | `₹ —` placeholder |
| Jharokha | Paneer Steam Momo (6 pcs), Classic French Fries, Cold Coffee | `₹ —` placeholder |
| Evening Edit | Veg Schezwan Momo (6 pcs), Chilli Garlic Noodles Veg | `₹ —` placeholder |

The individual **menu names are authentic to the supplied menu**, while final combo pricing, contact details, social handle, operating hours, and QR code intentionally remain placeholders for approval.

## Matching system

Both sides use the same divided Hawa Mahal / Tokyo skyline, a monogrammed momo-fold disc, a thin crop-mark frame, and a bottom placeholder strip. The front prioritizes invitation; the back prioritizes choice.
