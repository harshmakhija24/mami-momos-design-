## Print-art direction

The four designs will avoid stereotypical AI food visuals. Each will use intentional typography, limited colors, editable offer blocks, credible menu naming, and a distinct print composition. The Shark Tank line will be stated as **“Featured on Shark Tank India”** in accordance with the user’s provided claim, without using an unlicensed show logo.
